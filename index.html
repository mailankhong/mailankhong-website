AI content systems

An AI content system that still sounds like you

Most AI content sounds like nobody. In my post of 20 Aug 2026 I wrote it like this: "AI is not necessarily writing badly, but part of the blame lies in the prompts." So the machine I build starts before the first prompt.

Five files before the first post

The voice profile comes first, and it starts with a real conversation. From my post of 6 Aug 2026: "A Google Form asking "what is your tone?" has never once been answered truthfully."

Then the ICP, the content pillars and a grading profile. The brand guidelines come last, and they are for the visuals, not the writing.

  • Voice profile
  • ICP
  • Content pillars
  • Grading profile

Two machines

One looks for ideas. The other writes, and it starts with an interview, where you add the story only you know.

Three checks read every draft before it reaches you.

  1. Experience
  2. Idea
  3. Thinking
  4. AI
  5. Editing
  6. Judgment
  7. Publish

From my post of 20 Aug 2026: "AI belongs in the middle as a production step. It can never substitute for having something to say."
