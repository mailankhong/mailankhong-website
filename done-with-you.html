FAQ

Questions before the call.

Do I need to be technical to run this?
No. The stack runs inside Claude, with skills layered on top, and the APIs, integrations, and automations are pre-built. If you can describe what you want, you can run it.
What if I already have a content writer or agency?
Bring them in. The program teaches the system, and the system is what your writer uses. Most agencies are downstream of judgment and voice, so once those are in place, the existing team gets sharper, not displaced.
How much time does this take per week?
Plan for 3 hours per week during active phases: one hour of live working session, the rest spread across writing, engagement, and reviewing outputs.
What happens after Week 12?
You own the system. All artifacts (voice profile, skills, brand spec, ABM integration) sit in your environment, and a quarterly check-in is available if you want one. The program is designed for you to graduate, not subscribe.
Do I need to use Claude specifically?
Claude does the heaviest lifting because it is the strongest model for long-form judgment right now. Most clients run the system inside Claude. Other models in the stack are swappable for equivalents.
What if my team has in-house designers?
Use them. Expert mode plugs Figma and your designer into the same brand spec. Most clients use both DIY and Expert depending on the post.
Is this for me if I have no LinkedIn presence today?
Yes. Most clients start from a quiet account, and the 12 weeks are built around going from zero rhythm to a publishing operation. Starting fresh is sometimes easier than untangling years of mixed signals.
Can we onboard more than one person from my company?
Yes. Companies often onboard founders and several key employees together.
