How to Build an Employee-Generated Content Engine

Company Builds the Operating Layers
Voice Capture Editorial Layer Incentives Distribution Measurement
Founders & Execs

Voice type

  • Founder or 1–2 senior execs
  • Public POV, comfortable on podcasts and stage

Resources

  • Dedicated ghostwriter or editorial partner
  • Owned events, flagship narrative
  • Top-tier visual production

Funnel stage

  • Positioning, category definition, endorsement
Operators & Team

Voice type

  • AEs, engineers, RevOps, account managers
  • People doing the work daily, 10+ activatable

Resources

  • Voice capture from 30-min conversations, not forms
  • Editorial layer between draft and publish
  • Cash incentives, posting tools, training

Funnel stage

  • Proof posts, day-in-the-life, opinion takes
Network & Customers

Voice type

  • Customers who already won with you
  • Operator peers, category insiders

Resources

  • Access to product team, early launches
  • Exclusive data, behind-the-scenes
  • Asks framed as personal, not brand asks

Funnel stage

  • Testimonials, warm intros, case studies
