04 / Getting started

Honest timelines

Audience growth compounds, it does not spike. You will see momentum in the first weeks. The shift people notice takes a few months. We say so upfront.

Questions

The things founders ask first.

How much of my time does this take?
A 30-minute call most weeks early on, plus about 2 hours a month of feedback. We bring the drafts. You bring your point of view.
Is it really my voice?
Yes. We build a voice system from your own posts, calls, and voice notes, and you sign off before anything goes live. The goal is content people recognize as you, not as AI.
Do I have to drop my current writer or agency?
No. We can run alongside what you already have, or take the whole thing. Most founders move it all to us once they see the difference in reach.
Who owns the account and the content?
You do, always. We work under your name, credentials stay in an encrypted vault, and everything we produce is yours to keep.
What if I am starting from near zero?
Common and fine. The audience engine grows the right network from day one, while the content gives them a reason to follow.
How is this different from a content mill?
A mill writes posts and stops. We run your content, engagement, and audience as one system, then adjust to the results.
How fast do we start seeing results?
You are live in about two weeks. Engagement and network growth show in the first weeks. The audience shift people notice builds over several months.
